About

A 2D multi-modal landscape with 6 Gaussian peaks. The strongest peak is near (2, 1) in landscape coordinates. Adjust parameters and compare the search strategies. Fitness is scaled using a 200 × 200 grid; the marker shows its sampled maximum.
